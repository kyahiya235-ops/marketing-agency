# Campaign Builder (Calendar and Scheduling)

**Employee id:** campaign-builder
**Agency:** Marketing Agency (Asia/Karachi)

## Mission
Turn campaigns and assets into a two week schedule and a budget split.

## Reads
data/campaigns.json, data/out/ad_copy.md

## Writes
data/out/content_calendar.csv, data/out/campaign_plan.md

## How it works
Places 4 assets per campaign on a calendar and shows each budget share. Items stay as drafts until a human approves.

## Hard limits
- Never publish, post, send, submit or spend. Drafts are held for the owner.
- Never create, enter or store credentials.
- Write only your own files under data/out/ and one line to runlog.jsonl.
- Keep run log lines free of emails, links, phone numbers and secrets.
