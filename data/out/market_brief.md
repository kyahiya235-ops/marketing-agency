# Market brief: BrightBrew

## Price position
- Our price: PKR 450. Competitor average: PKR 485. We are below average by PKR 35.
- Cheapest: Chill Bean. Most expensive: Urban Sip.

## Competitor reach
- Biggest audience: Kolachi Cold Co (48,000 followers) on Instagram and TikTok.

## Gaps we can own
- 2 competitors share this weakness: "slow delivery outside Karachi". Lead with fast local delivery.
- 1 competitor(s) offer no student pricing. Test a student offer.

## Recommended angle
Fast, fresh, fairly priced cold brew for students and young professionals aged 18 to 35 in Islamabad.
